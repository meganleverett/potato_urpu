---
title: "Andean Cosmology"
---

 In Andean cosmology, potatoes were far more than a staple food, from which divine relations were bestowed upon these crops through the Incan Potato Mother, Axomamma, where ritualistic procedures were ingrained in the empire to reflect the centrality of the potatoes in the sustenance of the Andean people. They were sacred beings connected to the spiritual forces that governed the universe. Agricultural fertility was understood and associated with Pachamama (Mother Earth) and with the potato deity Axomama, who embodied the crop's life-giving power. Ritual offerings of potatoes accompanied planting and harvest cycles, as Andean myths and rituals often portrayed the deity as a living entity emerging from the underworld and interacting with celestial forces.![Sanchez Garrafa, Rodolfo. 2011. “Symbolism and Ritual Surrounding the Potato in the Andes”](https://meganleverett.github.io/potato_urpu/iiif/objects/symbolismandritual/symbolismandritual.jpg)
