 Furthermore, as per the centrality of potatoes in the sustenance of the Andean people, similar veneration was cast upon this crop in terms of religious practices that align this vessel with ritualistic matters. The divine relations of potatoes were bestowed upon by the Incan Potato Mother, Axomamma, who is the daughter of the Andean earth goddess Pachamama. Through the worship of these deities in meditative outlets such as household shrines and ritualistic practices, it revered the nourishment of the crop that maintained the diet of ordinary people in the Andes (Thurner &  Pimentel 248). Thus, the religious endeavors that solidify the cultural weight of the potato in Incan life, serves as a possible backdrop for the ritualistic implications of this Urpu.
